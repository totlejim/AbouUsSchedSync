Why We Built SchedSync

Like many schools, STI College Sta. Maria has long relied on manual scheduling methods—spreadsheets, paper, and extensive back-and-forth communication. Through our research, we found that creating a single semester schedule takes administrators anywhere from one to two weeks, and sometimes up to a full month.

We also learned that teachers and students often receive schedule changes late—or not at all—leading to confusion, missed classes, and wasted time.

So we asked ourselves: What if scheduling could be simpler, faster, and more accurate?

That question became SchedSync.

What SchedSync Does

SchedSync is a web-based scheduling system designed specifically for STI College Sta. Maria. It helps administrators create organized, conflict-free schedules while giving teachers and students real-time access to the information they need.

Our system focuses on three main goals:

Reducing Conflicts

Automatically detects overlapping teacher assignments and double-booked rooms before schedules are published.

Enhancing Efficiency

Suggests available teachers, free rooms, or alternative time slots for faster and smarter scheduling decisions.

Improving Transparency

Provides all users with a clear, real-time view of schedules, room assignments, and faculty availability.

Key Features at a Glance

Automatic Conflict Detection

Instantly flags overlapping classes or double-booked resources as the schedule is being built.

Automatic Suggestions

Recommends available teachers, free rooms, or alternative time slots when conflicts arise.

Faculty Page

A dedicated view where teachers can see their weekly class schedule from a single, reliable source.

Campus Page

A campus-wide view of all room schedules, showing class and teacher assignments throughout the week.

Upcoming Events Notifications

Alerts users about scheduled events and room reservations so everyone stays informed.

Real-Time Updates & Download

Changes are visible immediately to all users. Schedules can also be downloaded as PNG, PDF, or Excel files.

Who Can Use SchedSync

👔

Administrators

Have full access to create, edit, and publish schedules. They can manage rooms, assign teachers, post event notifications, and view both Faculty and Campus pages.

👨‍🏫

Teachers

Cannot edit schedules but can view their assigned classes on the Faculty Page and leave comments to communicate with administrators.

🎓

Students

Have view-only access. They can see class schedules, room assignments, and teacher availability on the Campus Page.
